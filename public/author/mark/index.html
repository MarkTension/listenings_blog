Jeremiah Chiu & Sofia Honer - Different Rooms

Music-wise, this time of the year is my favorite time.
It’s all thanks to the end of the year lists that give me a proper tailwind.
One of these lists, brought my attention to Jeremiah Chiu & Sofia Honer’s Different Rooms.

For some reason, I completely missed this release, even though I absolutely loved their previous work, Recordings from the Åland Islands.

Different Rooms goes deep. It doesn’t force a specific mood too much, but if it does it’s kind of nostalgic, and psychedelic. The songs are like an ocean of synthesizer and acoustic instruments (often violin (a little subdued, not the annoying kind)), and the vibe is sort of implicitly rhythmic, almost hypnotic, through repeating motifs, not percussion per se.

It’s super cool how this album just came out, but what a time-machine it really is. On the first listen I got transported into my favorite trip-hop-soundtracked 90’s high octane movies.
The sound is reminiscent of Massive Attack, Board of Canada, Portishead, and some Moby in the mix. Still it’s doing its own authentic thing with it. Hope it’s a sign that trip-hop is making a comeback!

Skee Mask - Compro

Skee Mask is known for a bunch of amazing releases on Ilian Tape. This is his second album, and it’s incredible.

The cover says a lot; Misty, obscured, and deep. It’s easy to get lost in the sauce with the lush atmospheric sounds. The beats keep things tightly together though. It has a sort of dub-techno, ambient techno feel, but at the same time this meticulous hip-hop style chopped-up beats give it some real magic.

Ike Zwanikken - Greed Gave Me A Lily

Now why I love this album so much might be the same reason as why I’m blown away by David Lynch movies; They never fully disclose the situation. There’s just a deep unknown with different forces at play, which I’ll never understand. I’m just somewhere on the surface of it, blissfully being pulled along, meandering between scary / loving / funny.

Not that it’s the same vibe, but this album does the same thing to me. It’s warm, nostalgic, weirdly alien, and then loving. Just new worlds to explore. There’s nothing really like it.

Mr YT - Brand New Day

Today’s listening is Mr YT (Yuji Takenouchi)’s Brand New Day.

The first song, ‘Morning’, is just unbelievable. Such a journey. ’Nite’ is a close second. For both it’s quite hard to put a finger on which time they really belongs in. Could be because Takenouchi scored some iconic and futuristic video games for Sony, which must’ve conjured up otherwordly scenes in his mind on a daily basis.

The other songs are cool housey danceable tunes, which hint at the album’s creation time (1997) way better. Also very cool.
