Fabiano do Nascimento - Vila

Stef

October 31, 2026

When my head is full of thoughts, I put on this album. The jazzy, orchestral melodies and soothing Brazilian vocals ease my mind. When I first heard Vila, I thought it was a reissue of an old Brazilian record, from Azymuth or Jorge Ben’s generation. Turns out 43-year old Fabiano from Rio de Janeiro just has great voice, and the Vila was actually first released on Far Out Recordings in February this year. An instant classic, I would say.

Edit: At the moment of posting this blog, I realise Berry recommended a different album of the same artist last week. I guess my subconscious was playing along. Listening to both albums now, I find it inspiring to hear how collaborations can make such a difference. Compare for yourself!

~ Stef
