Fabiano Do Nascimento & E Ruscha V - Aquáticos

Berry

October 24, 2026

Aquáticos came out in January this year and is for sure going to end up on my top albums of the year. It’s a collaboration between Brazilian guitarist Fabiano do Nascimento and American electronic musician E Ruscha V, brought together by (and released on) the excellent Amsterdam label Music From Memory.

I got to see Fabiano live last March, and his virtuous playing is so, so inventive - although deeply rooted in Brazilian musical traditions as well as the wider umbrellas of jazz and classical guitar music, he plays his 7-string and 9-string guitar in ways I haven’t seen before. I was particularly mesmerized by a tool he used that dampened his strings, giving each note a percussive slap and shorter duration (the effect can be heard best on the track Leaves on this album, as well as the excellent album opener Nascer).

Combining his playing and composing with the electronics of E Ruscha V makes this a far-cry from a ‘guitar’ album. The end result is much more reminiscent of chilled-out 90s videogames - particularly the underwater levels, because unsurprisingly, the sound and vibe and atmosphere of water are a central, recurring theme on this record titled Aquáticos. This is one of those albums that clearly ticks the boxes of ‘ambient music’, but with a forward drive that is both relaxed and energetic. Pure alchemy.

~ Berry
