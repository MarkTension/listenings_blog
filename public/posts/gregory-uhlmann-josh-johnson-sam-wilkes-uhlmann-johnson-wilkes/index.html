Gregory Uhlmann, Josh Johnson, Sam Wilkes - Uhlmann Johnson Wilkes

Mark

April 4, 2026

Happy Easter folks! I’m happy to share that today’s Listening fits the Easter holiday theme quite well. It’s described as ‘a jazz-informed take on progressive electro-acoustic chamber music’ and could be something that you might play on the couch with the family these days. For me it’s surely been the winding-down music for the weekend afternoons the last few months.

While the soundscapes could take some space sometimes, (many parts are live-improvisations), the warmth and beauty you get from some sections is unparalleled. They land on these very pretty, intimate sections of warm melodies, played so softly.

A premature ending of the listening session forced by a respective family member is worth the risk. In case that happens, insist on playing one the last song, The Fool on the Hill.

~ Mark
