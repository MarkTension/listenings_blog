John Talabot - Fin

Stef

June 1, 2024

Two weeks ago on Lentekabinet, I was somehow dissapointed to find out John Talabot had jumped on the trance train that has been riding for a bit too long. Along with DJ’s such as Job Jobse and Young Marco, Talabot is speeding way beyond the 130 limit.

I know I sound like a grumpy old man now…

Luckily, at home I can play the low BPMs of 2011’s release ‘Fin’. A true classic in it’s genre, according to many of my dancefloor friends. It takes me back to the days my feet could still meet the beat and makes me hopeful for a downtempo revival after the superfast music hype is over.

~ Stef
