Yesterday I went to Concerto record store for Personal Trainer’s album release with fellow Listenings writer Berry. I saw a tight band with great chemistry playing slacker indie rock songs with joy. I knew about Personal Trainer but had not listened to their music much. However, they instantly grabbed my attention and I’ve been replaying their new album. Personal highlight is the upbeat track ‘[Moping](https://open.spotify.com/track/4PI5GBWxgefPpyTgAwzfiN?si=5e491593606b454a)’, with the witty lyrics ‘professionally moping around again’. For those excited about seeing them live, in two weeks time they will play in [Carré](https://carre.nl/voorstelling/personal-trainer)!
