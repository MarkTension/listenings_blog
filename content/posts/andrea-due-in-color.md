When I first saw the album cover, I remembered taking a similar photo at the exact same place: Joshua Tree Park, Cholla cactus garden. More relevant: I remember driving the long desert road with the car speakers blasting Four Tet’s New Energy.  
  
Maybe my mind played tricks on me by associating the images, but I really think the that Due in Color hits that exact same note for me.  
  
The vibes are quite similar: Both great electronic music albums. Ambient mixed with really good rhythmical tracks, and feels like mysterious landscapes opening up when listening to it.

Also a big fan how the drums seem to be live-drumming for a big part. Sounds all very much alive.   
  


~ Mark
