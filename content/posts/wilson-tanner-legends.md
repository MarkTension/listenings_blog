While writing this, I’m in that hazy state of happiness and tiredness, as it’s the Monday after three days of Best Kept Secret festival. I’m re-watching the concert registrations on my phone, while lying in sun. Little kids are jumping in the water next to my house, and I’m about to follow. The soundtrack that fits my mood is the latest album of Wilson Tanner. You might know one of the duo, under the name of Andras. Together the two Aussies create peaceful ambient, that makes you feel like being on a vineyard in rural Perth. 

This Thursdays the two mates will be playing at [Murmur](https://murmurmur.nl/events/1156/) in Amsterdam! Who’s joining?
