I found out about Hannes because Benny Sings once said in an interview he’s a big admirer. I think the artists show a lot of resemblance, they're both great at writing little pop songs. 

God Bless Sweden came out last month, but contains some of Hannes' earlier work — the popular song [Stockholmsvy](https://open.spotify.com/track/3ibfopvKlRMxGC6kMFhcBt?si=1ab520058bbd49d3) was released in 2022. Please don't be misled by the intense intro tune, and be sure to play the second half of the album where hits are.

I’m going on a Sweden trip this summer with fellow editors of this newsletter, and God Bless Sweden seems to be the perfect soundtrack for it.  

 ~ Stef
