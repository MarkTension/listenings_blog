_"I got sugar for your monkeeheeheehee"_  
  
That line is stuck in my head since a dear friend send me the track _Sugar_ by Mytron some weeks ago. Not knowing the artist, I played the entire album and been revisiting it regularly. Both the lyrics and the music sound like someone having a lot of fun making music, not taking life too seriously while still delivering great dance music in a dark space disco style that reminds me of Axel Boman. Another seemingly random but convincing tune is the title track _Propeller_ that includes the German lyrics: “ _Wollen sie ein Flugzeug sein, geben Sie Propeller ein.”_  
  
Looking up the London-based Mytron up on [Bandcamp](https://mytron.bandcamp.com/album/propeller), I find his own description hilariously accurate: 

_Creating finely fused psychedelic journeys through groove, pausing for stopovers in rainforest  
rhythm, intergalactic electronica and dubbed out disco._  
  
\- Stef
