![For Those of You Who Have Never \(And Also Those Who Have\) - Wikipedia](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/3/32/ForThoseOfYou_HurecoS_cover.jpg)

  
For almost a month I’ve mainly been listening to [dub-techno mixes](https://www.youtube.com/driftdeeper). They’re great, but also symptomatic of spending too little time to discover new stuff.  
  
For the past week though, I’ve been playing this really interesting album “For Those Of You Who Have Never (And Also Those Who Have)” on repeat.   
This album was always half skipped-over when I just discovered the musical corner where ambient / experimental intersects with club music from labels like Peak Oil, West Mineral ltd, 3XL.  
  
It’s an ambient album and needs a couple of listens, but when properly invested, it kind of unlocks a portal to a dream world.  
  
So happy I found it again, and feels like revitalizing my curiosity for music again. 

~ Mark
