Now why I love this album so much might be the same reason as why I'm blown away by David Lynch movies; They never fully disclose the situation. There's just a deep unknown with different forces at play, which I'll never understand. I'm just somewhere on the surface of it, blissfully being pulled along, meandering between scary / loving / funny. 

Not that it's the same vibe, but this album does the same thing to me. It's warm, nostalgic, weirdly alien, and then loving. Just new worlds to explore. There's nothing really like it.

Big shout-out to Griff ([Aspetuck](https://aspetuck.bandcamp.com/?from=search&search_item_id=3680072020&search_item_type=b&search_match_part=%3F&search_page_id=3994956603&search_page_no=1&search_rank=1&search_sig=7fc27a851606099218bc5cbea94d4fde)) for recommending it to me.
