![Florian T M Zeisig - Planet Inc. Bleep.](https://d1rgjmn2wmqeif.cloudfront.net/r/g/435406.jpg)   


For some reason, a couple of mornings ago, I had Planet Inc playing on my iPhone speaker, and forgot all about it while continuing tinkering on a side-project. It was magical. Only when the album finished I realized what a deep trip I was in. Listening to music without knowing that I was, was like my head-space made it by itself. It made me think of the movie Inception; conveying an idea while 'unconscious' can convey the strongest message.

Anyway, I love this album. Trippy, dubby, and wonderfully ambient. Feels like being an amoeba floating around in a primordial soup.
