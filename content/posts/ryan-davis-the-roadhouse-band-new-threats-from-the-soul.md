Last Wednesday, MJ Lenderman & The Wind played a stellar show in Amsterdam. As I suspect many of you are already familiar with his tiptop record [Manning Fireworks](https://open.spotify.com/album/1bKiiYGt1fzm9YMlTnUqpN?si=86TaoqrrSc64Qx-w-pYlBw) (if not, go check it out), today’s newsletter is about the equally impressive support act, Ryan Davis & The Roadhouse Band. 

 

[As touring becomes more expansive and more of a hassle](https://hmc.chartmetric.com/why-artists-arent-touring-2025/), it is a great privilege to see a support act bring a six-piece band to the stage, and an excellent one at that. Ryan Davis’ vocals are reminiscent of a less angry Matt Berninger while the band’s sound could be described as Songs: Ohia-inspired with the rhythm section of The War on Drugs. It is a pleasure to hear a band that sounds so comfortable on stage, a vibe they managed to hold on their latest album, recommended here today.

 

If you need more convincing: Silver Jews frontman David Berman once said Ryan Davis is the best non-rapper lyricist he knows. 

 

By the way, for those in Amsterdam – [find Ryan Davis and his Roadhouse Band at Tolhuistuin on September 7](https://www.paradiso.nl/programma/ryan-davis-the-roadhouse-band/2612993).
