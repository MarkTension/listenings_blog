That late summer feeling. Sad about the summer passing, still some occasional dips in the lake, and a pulling inwards.  
  
Musically it brings me to a sound with a somewhat specific emotional tone. My first time consciously finding a soundtrack for this feeling was Cass McCombs [Mangy Love](https://cassmccombs.bandcamp.com/album/mangy-love). Another year it was [At Scaramouche](https://shabasonandkrgovich.bandcamp.com/album/at-scaramouche?search_item_id=2585902156&search_item_type=a&search_match_part=%3F&search_page_id=5772162182&search_page_no=0&search_rank=1) by Joseph Shabason & Krgovich.  
  
I think this September blessed me with finding a perfect soundtrack for it too: Spacious Heart by Anthony Calonico. It is ambient jazz, sincere and makes me feel like I’m driving back from the lake at dusk. Mostly instrumental, but warm vocals as well. I discovered it through Calonico’s earlier collaboration as [Total Blue](https://music-from-memory.bandcamp.com/album/total-blue), which is similar, but also has a 90's crime thriller / cop drama film vibe (super recommended too). Anyway, I've been really enjoying Spacious Heart and I hope you will too.

~ Mark
